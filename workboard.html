workboard

Design the process with your client — right where you discuss it.

The Workboard is a canvas for planning a Pardot process together. Drop in the assets that already exist — with their real names, straight from the audit — or sketch new ones you still have to build. Wire the logic, save the board, and open it again whenever you review with the customer.

what it's for

A shared picture before anything is built.

Pardot processes are hard to talk through in the abstract. The Workboard turns that conversation into something you and the client can see, change and agree on — then keep as the reference while it gets built.

Real or planned assets

Add existing Pardot assets with their correct names, pulled from the audit — or add new, planned components specified by type, for the pieces that don't exist yet.

Build the logic

Connect the cards with decisions, waits, branches and stops — the whole flow, laid out the way you'd actually explain it at the table.

Saved & ready to review

Every board is saved, so you can come back to it in the next session with the customer — and extend it with notes and screenshots as the plan firms up.

where it fits

Next to the processes it describes.

The Workboard lives inside parAdots, alongside the Engagement Studio view and process discovery — so planning happens in the same place you read the real org. Use it to lay out a new flow from scratch, or to reconstruct and verify an Engagement Studio program you want to walk through with the client.
