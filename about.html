about

The consultant behind parAdots.

I'm Nadya Higazi — a Salesforce and Marketing Cloud Account Engagement (Pardot) consultant. parAdots grew out of the day-to-day work of auditing real B2B marketing estates, so it reads the way a practitioner actually thinks about them.

I work as a freelance Salesforce & Pardot (MCAE) consultant. Most of my clients are B2B building-products and industrial manufacturers in the DACH region, where marketing and sales rarely run in a straight line — they run through a three-tier distribution model (dreistufiger Vertrieb): manufacturer, wholesale, and trade. A lot of my work is making marketing automation fit that reality rather than fighting it.

Before going independent, I spent two decades in international software and project delivery at the Goethe-Institut, latterly as regional project lead (OSKA-Beauftragte) for software rollouts across 9 countries and 17 institutes in North Africa and the Middle East. Coordinating a rollout at that scale — many sites, many stakeholders, one system that had to work for all of them — is the same muscle I now use on marketing-automation programs.

parAdots came directly out of that consulting work. Auditing a Pardot and Salesforce setup by hand is slow, easy to get wrong, and impossible to hand over cleanly. So I built a suite that collects the whole estate, explains every asset in plain language, maps the dependencies, and flags what's broken or non-compliant — the audit I wished I'd had on day one of every new client.
